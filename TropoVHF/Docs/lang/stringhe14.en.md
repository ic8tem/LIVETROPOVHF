## 6

How to trust the expected gain: high, medium or low. It is a sum of points, not a probability: agreement between the models (loss planned against P.452 with the conduits and parabolic equation, baseline against ITM: up to 3 dB +1, over 8 dB −1), advance (within 24 hours +1, over 72 −1), continuity of the layer on the route (70% +1, under 30% −1) and its persistence (+1), verification of the GFS with the nearest radio wave (±1) and live confirmation along the route in the last hour and a half gain (cluster, PSKReporter, APRS, FM-DX: 1–2 High from 3 points, average from 0 to 2, low below zero. Provision manual, § 10 septies.
